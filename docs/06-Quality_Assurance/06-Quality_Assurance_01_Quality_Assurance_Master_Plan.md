# 1. Purpose

This document defines the complete Quality Assurance strategy governing the TaskMyScreen project.

Its objective is to guarantee that every delivery is production-ready, regression-safe, deterministic and fully aligned with the functional specifications.

Quality is not a phase.

Quality is embedded into every implementation.

---

# 2. QA Philosophy

TaskMyScreen follows a preventive quality model.

The priority is not to detect bugs.

The priority is to prevent bugs.

The hierarchy is:

Architecture

↓

Business Rules

↓

Implementation

↓

Testing

↓

Monitoring

↓

Continuous Improvement

# 8. Business Rule Validation

QA shall verify:

One task generates one reminder.

No duplicated reminders.

Premium users can reschedule.

Free users cannot reschedule.

Premium users have unlimited tasks.

Free users respect monthly limits.

Notification image always matches the selected task.
